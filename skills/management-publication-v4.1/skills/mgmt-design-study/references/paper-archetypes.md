# Paper Archetypes

## 1. Theory-testing quantitative
Core: established/extended theory → hypotheses → measured constructs → statistical test.
Do not confuse model complexity with theoretical contribution.

## 2. Causal-identification
Core: important causal question → counterfactual → identification → mechanism/boundary.
Causal language requires design support.

## 3. Phenomenon-driven
Core: surprising/important phenomenon → academic puzzle → theoretical explanation → boundary.
Do not causalize a surprising correlation automatically.

## 4. Text-measurement/substantive
Core: hard-to-observe management construct → validated measurement → substantive finding.
AI/LLM is usually measurement infrastructure, not the management contribution.

## 5. Configurational
Core: necessity/sufficiency/asymmetry/equifinality.
Use NCA/QCA only when the theoretical relation is actually configurational.

## 6. Longitudinal process/case
Core: sequence, turning points, actor actions, process mechanism.
Methods may include temporal bracketing, process tracing, comparative cases, negative cases, triangulation.
Do not force regression or estimands when the research question is processual.

## 7. Stylized-fact/dynamic
Core: a theoretically consequential empirical regularity or long-run pattern → contrast with existing belief → explanation/boundary.
Description must alter a meaningful belief, not merely report differences.

## 8. Formal/decision/optimization
Core: a management decision problem → formal assumptions/model → propositions/algorithm/solution → empirical or computational validation as appropriate.
Do not disguise an engineering optimization task as theory unless the management decision contribution is explicit.
