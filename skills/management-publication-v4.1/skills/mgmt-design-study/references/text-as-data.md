# Text-as-Data Management Design

## Document meaning first
Define institutionally what the document represents before modeling.

Examples:
- annual work report may reflect agenda/work priorities;
- formal regulation may reflect adoption/authorization;
- enforcement record may reflect implementation;
- media text may reflect communication.

Never silently move between these meanings.

## Measurement contract
Specify:
1. corpus and missingness;
2. text unit;
3. construct taxonomy;
4. annotation rules;
5. human validation;
6. class-wise error;
7. temporal comparability;
8. model/encoder sensitivity;
9. aggregation rule;
10. interpretation of zeros/missingness.

## Relative shares
If shares sum to one, distinguish absolute intensity from relative composition.
A fall in share is not automatically an absolute policy decline.

## AI/LLM
Use AI because it solves a measurement/scale problem relevant to the research question.
Validate it. Do not assume methodological novelty from model names.
