# Theory Provenance

For every mechanism, mark one:

## ESTABLISHED
The mechanism is directly grounded in an existing theory/stream.
Requirement: verified source and faithful interpretation.

## EXTENSION
The mechanism adapts an established explanation to a new condition or adds one step.
Requirement: state exactly what is inherited and what is new.

## INDUCTIVE
The mechanism is developed from the present data/process evidence.
Requirement: do not present it as prior theory; show how evidence supports the emergent explanation.

Do not invent a named theory or mechanism to make the paper sound sophisticated.
