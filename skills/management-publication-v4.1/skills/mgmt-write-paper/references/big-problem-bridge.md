# Big-Problem Bridge for Writing

Before writing the opening, complete:

1. Big problem:
2. Management mechanism linking it to our study:
3. Core construct:
4. Empirical measure:
5. Main evidence:
6. What the evidence can legitimately imply for the big problem:

If any link is missing, lower the opening claim.

Do not use a national strategy, fashionable concept, or broad societal goal as a decorative first sentence unless the paper's mechanism and evidence genuinely bear on it.
