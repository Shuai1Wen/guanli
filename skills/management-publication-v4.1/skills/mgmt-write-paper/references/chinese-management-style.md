# Mature Chinese Management Writing

## Abstract
重大问题 → design → core findings on one line → theory/real implication.

## Introduction
P1 important problem  
P2 existing knowledge  
P3 real unresolved issue  
P4 why it matters  
P5 why our study can answer  
P6 concentrated study/design/findings/contribution

## Results
Why test this?  
How is the table/specification arranged?  
What is the result?  
What is its magnitude/meaning?  
What does it change in the paper's argument?

## Conclusion
Direct answer → literature update → management/policy implication.

## Sentence rules
Prefer `明确主体 + 明确动作 + 必要对象/条件 + 结果`.

Reduce:
- abstract noun chains;
- "赋能/重塑/跃迁/深层逻辑" without defined constructs;
- excessive 进行/构建/形成/实现;
- stacked "层面/维度/视角";
- algorithm-paper translation.

The problem should still sound important after removing fashionable vocabulary.
