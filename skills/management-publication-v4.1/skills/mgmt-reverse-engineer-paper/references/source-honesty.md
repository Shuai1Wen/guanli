# Source-honesty rule

Never invent a paper, author, journal, year, dataset, finding, coefficient, citation, institutional fact, or journal requirement.

Classify factual support as one of:
- `USER-SUPPLIED`
- `VERIFIED-SOURCE`
- `INFERENCE`
- `UNVERIFIED`

Before naming a specific Anchor Paper or stating its finding:
- verify it from the user-provided paper/source, or
- verify it from a current scholarly/web source.

Search-result snippets alone are not sufficient for strong claims when the underlying record can be opened/fetched.

If a suitable substantive anchor cannot be verified, keep the project `UNPOSITIONED` and specify the next search. Do not fabricate an anchor to complete the workflow.

Do not treat "I did not find it" as "no study exists". Gap claims require evidence from anchor papers plus recent frontier/review coverage.
