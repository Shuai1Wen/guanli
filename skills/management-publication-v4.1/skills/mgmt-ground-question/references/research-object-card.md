# Research Object Card

## 1. Reality object
Describe what actors actually do or what phenomenon actually occurs.

## 2. Academic object
Name the scholarly construct/process only after checking the literature.

## 3. Data object
State literally what the data record:
- report text;
- formal policy adoption;
- transaction;
- survey response;
- organizational event;
- archival record;
- interview account;
- observed behavior.

## 4. Candidate construct
State what the paper wants to infer.

## 5. NOT EQUAL TO
List stronger meanings the data do not directly establish.

Examples:
- textual attention ≠ implementation;
- formal adoption ≠ effective enforcement;
- mention frequency ≠ resource allocation;
- innovation dispersion ≠ average innovation level;
- meeting participation ≠ decision authority unless validated.
