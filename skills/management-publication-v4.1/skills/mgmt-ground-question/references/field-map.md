# Academic Mountain Map

Do not return only names and citations.

## Central problem
What does this literature fundamentally try to explain?

## Stable knowledge
Which claims have accumulated credible support?

## Active disagreements / bottlenecks
Where do theories, findings, measurements, or identification strategies still disagree?

## Evolution
How has the question changed over time?

## Evidence tradition
What units, data, and methods are normal, and why?

## Frontier
What have the strongest recent papers already done?

## Our landing point
Name the 1–3 papers/streams our paper would directly talk to.

## Adjacent but not equivalent
List tempting neighboring literatures and explain the construct difference.
