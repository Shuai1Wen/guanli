# Anchor Ledger

Classify every anchor:

- `SUBSTANTIVE` — studies the same core management problem/relationship/process.
- `THEORY` — supplies the theoretical mechanism but may study a different phenomenon.
- `METHOD` — supplies research design or analytical method.
- `STYLE` — exemplifies target-journal architecture or prose.

Rules:
1. `POSITIONED` requires at least one verified `SUBSTANTIVE` anchor for an unpositioned project.
2. A METHOD anchor cannot substitute for the substantive academic market.
3. Verify the exact paper and finding before naming it.
4. Deep-read the closest substantive anchor before freezing novelty.
